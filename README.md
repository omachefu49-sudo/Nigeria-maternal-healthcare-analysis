# Maternal Healthcare in Nigeria: Socioeconomic Disparities & Service Utilization Analysis

**Project Status:** 🟡 Planned

An independent public health data analytics project investigating socioeconomic and geographical disparities in maternal healthcare access and service utilization across Nigeria.

## 1. Problem Statement

Access to maternal healthcare services remains uneven across socioeconomic and geographical groups in Nigeria.

Differences in household wealth, educational attainment and place of residence may influence access to antenatal care, skilled birth attendance and other essential maternal health services.

This project seeks to identify underserved populations and examine the factors associated with disparities in maternal healthcare utilization.

## 2. Research Objectives

- Examine patterns of antenatal care utilization across Nigeria.
- Assess access to skilled birth attendance.
- Investigate socioeconomic and geographical disparities.
- Examine the relationship between education, household wealth and maternal healthcare utilization.
- Identify populations with comparatively low service utilization.
- Generate evidence-based recommendations for improving healthcare access.

## 3. Data Sources

- [Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey (NDHS)](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/3540)
- [World Health Organization (WHO)](https://www.who.int/data/gho)
- [World Bank Open Data](https://data.worldbank.org/country/nigeria)
- [Federal Ministry of Health – MSDAT](https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/msdat/)

## 4. Analytical Approach

- Exploratory data analysis.
- Descriptive and comparative statistics.
- Socioeconomic and geographical segmentation.
- Cross-tabulation and chi-square analysis.
- Logistic regression.
- Multivariable analysis.
- Identification of underserved populations.
